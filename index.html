Introduction to the Types of Art

Art is a way for people to express their thoughts, feelings, ideas, and imagination. It has been an important part of human life for thousands of years and can be found in many different forms. The main types of art includes visual arts, performing arts, literary arts, and applied arts Visual arts include painting and drawing Performing arts include theatre while literary arts include poetry, stories, and other forms of writing. Applied arts, such as fashion and design, combine creativity with practical purposes. Although these types of art are different, they all help people communicate, create, and understand the world in meaningful ways.
